# Chile Tourism Data Analytics & Decision Strategy

## Overview

An end-to-end analytics and decision science project evaluating tourism competitiveness across Chile's regions and translating data into investment, forecasting and resource allocation decisions.

The project progresses through diagnostic, predictive and prescriptive analytics and combines Excel, Tableau, forecasting and optimization.

## Business Problem

How should Chile prioritize tourism development across its regions while balancing visitor demand, infrastructure readiness, digital capability, sustainability, regional equity and capacity constraints?

## 1. Diagnostic Analytics

A regional competitiveness framework was developed using historical tourism indicators, recent demand recovery and digital readiness.

The analysis classified regions into three strategic groups:

**Scale Now**  
Regions positioned for immediate investment and promotion.

**Prepare First**  
Regions requiring targeted improvements before broad promotion.

**Wait**  
Regions where foundational constraints should be addressed before national promotion.

### Methods

• Data cleaning and standardization  
• Composite scoring  
• Indicator normalization  
• Sensitivity analysis  
• Regional benchmarking  
• Traveler segmentation  
• Digital readiness analysis  

## 2. Predictive Analytics

Quarterly forecasting models were developed and evaluated against a seasonal naive benchmark.

Rather than treating forecasts as automatically reliable, the project uses holdout testing and RMSE comparison to determine when a model should influence decisions.

### Methods

• Time series forecasting  
• Trend and seasonality modeling  
• Holdout validation  
• RMSE evaluation  
• Benchmark comparison  
• Scenario planning  
• Model governance  

## 3. Prescriptive Analytics

Two optimization models were developed to translate analytical insights into resource allocation decisions.

### Tourism Development Fund

Optimization of a USD 45 million regional tourism development fund across regions and investment programs.

### High Season Visitor Allocation

Optimization of visitor routing subject to gateway, accommodation and regional capacity constraints.

### Methods

• Linear optimization  
• Resource allocation  
• Constraint modeling  
• Scenario analysis  
• Sensitivity analysis  
• Shadow price interpretation  
• Capacity planning  

## Key Decision Insights

The analysis demonstrates that tourism investment should not be distributed uniformly across regions.

Regional strategy should reflect differences in demand, infrastructure, digital readiness, tourism assets, execution capacity and sustainability constraints.

The optimization analysis further demonstrates that physical capacity can become more restrictive than available funding, particularly in high-value tourism regions.
